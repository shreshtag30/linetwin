Track 4: DigitalTwin.ai Solution Architecture

This prototype demonstrates a full discrete-event digital twin of a 30-station automotive assembly line, specifically addressing all brief solutioning areas:

  • Laplacian Harmonic Extension (B3/A1): 8 of 30 stations are uninstrumented (dark). Their state is inferred live with provable partition-of-unity attribution.
  • Active Period Method (B2): Continuous active streak tracking validated against sensitivity ground truth (100% top-1 accuracy).
  • Model B Defect Prediction (B2/A2): Monotone XGBoost + isotonic calibration with TreeSHAP attributions labeled associative (not causal).
  • Defect Genealogy (A4): Reversing unit transfer delays back to likely origin station on downstream final inspection detection.
  • Multi-Stakeholder Lens (A5/B4): Floor Supervisor, Plant Manager, and Leadership views driven by the same unified underlying model.
  • Zero-Downtime OT Integration (B5/A3): Read-only telemetry tap via OPC-UA / historian with no PLC modification risk.
